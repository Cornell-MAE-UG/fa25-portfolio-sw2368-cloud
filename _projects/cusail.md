## Hull form

I worked the hull's hydrostatics in DELFTship, targeting prismatic coefficient and longitudinal center of buoyancy and using Lackenby transforms to shift the form toward those targets.

## CFD

I converted the STL geometry to CAD for meshing and set up the flow simulation in ANSYS Fluent, using the k-ω SST turbulence model with boundary conditions for water.

## Manufacturing

I led the mechanical subteam through hull and deck manufacturing using resin infusion and composite layup, working from SolidWorks and DELFTship models. I also machine aluminum parts on the team's ProtoTRAK KMX, with toolpaths from Fusion 360 CAM.
